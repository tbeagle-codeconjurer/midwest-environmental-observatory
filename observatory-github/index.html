Review method and limitations
Regional data are grouped by station, source family, metric and units. Baseline: Nov 22–24, 2026 Central time; each observed hour contributes its median. At least 24 distinct baseline hours and nonzero median absolute deviation (MAD) are required. Latest observation is compared with the baseline median using 0.67448975 × (value − median) / MAD. Absolute score ≥ 3.5 is a descriptive review flag, not a validated statistical test or health standard. No adjustment for seasonality, time of day, weather or multiple comparisons. Wind direction and precipitation are excluded. Stale series (over 3 hours) are not scored. Xact nonzero values in the last 24 hours are listed separately; no pre-event baseline or detection limits are assumed. Official alert records require a recent archive check and unexpired alerts. The dashboard refreshes while open; it does not send notifications or replace emergency warning systems.
